Curated references for this module. Each entry is a primary or widely-cited source. Books with multiple editions move; always check publication date and current edition against the content as it existed when this file was written (2026-11).

## Canonical engineering-management reading (prerequisite for this module)

The general management canon is `ai-augmented-team-manager` (level 40) territory and is this module's prerequisite, not its content. The references below are the canonical reading the manager is assumed to have internalised; this module specialises them to the AI engineering surface.

- Fournier, Camille — *The Manager's Path: A Guide for Tech Leaders Navigating Growth and Change*. O'Reilly, 2017. [O'Reilly page](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/the-managers-path/9781491973882/) — the canonical shape of the engineering-management career ladder, the 1:1 practice, the tech-lead and manager-of-managers transitions, and the cross-level altitude read this module's chapter 06 adapts.
- Grove, Andrew S. — *High Output Management*. Vintage, 1995 (reissued 2015). [Vintage page](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/58715/high-output-management-by-andrew-s-grove/) — the operating-system substrate underneath the ritual set this module designs (the weekly staff meeting, the OKR cadence, task-relevant maturity).
- Larson, Will — *An Elegant Puzzle: Systems of Engineering Management*. Stripe Press, 2019. [Stripe Press page](https://press.stripe.com/an-elegant-puzzle) — the team-sizing framework this module's chapter 02 specialises, the span-of-control reading, the systems-thinking altitude.
- Larson, Will — *Staff Engineer: Leadership Beyond the Management Track*. 2021. [staffeng.com](https://staffeng.com/) — the staff-plus IC altitude the chapter 06 partner-needed direction reads against; the tech-lead vs. staff-plus distinction.
- Lopp, Michael (Rands) — *Managing Humans: Biting and Humorous Tales of a Software Engineering Manager*. 4th edition, Apress, 2019. [Apress page](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4842-7115-2) · *Rands in Repose* blog archive. [randsinrepose.com](https://randsinrepose.com/)
- Hogan, Lara — *Resilient Management*. A Book Apart, 2019. [A Book Apart page](https://abookapart.com/products/resilient-management) — manager-of-managers craft, growth-plan surface, the first-time-manager coaching this module's chapter 06 adapts.

## AI-assistant empirical research (chapter 05 honest measurement)

The literature on frontier-assistant effects on developer productivity, judgment, and craft. The field is young; findings differ across populations and tasks. Read broadly and update as the evidence evolves.

- Peng, Sida, Kalliamvakou, Eirini et al. — *The Impact of AI on Developer Productivity: Evidence from GitHub Copilot*. 2023. [arXiv:2302.06590](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.06590) — the original RCT study; a 55.8% task-completion speedup on a specific JavaScript HTTP-server task on a specific developer sample. Important primary reading; chapter 05 treats the finding as task-specific, not team-level.
- Dell'Acqua, Fabrizio, McFowland, Edward, Mollick, Ethan R., Lifshitz-Assaf, Hila, Kellogg, Katherine, Rajendran, Saran, Krayer, Lisa, Candelon, François, and Lakhani, Karim R. — *Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier: Field Experimental Evidence of the Effects of AI on Knowledge Worker Productivity and Quality*. Harvard Business School Working Paper 24-013, September 2023. [HBS link](https://www.hbs.edu/faculty/Pages/item.aspx?num=64700) — the "jagged frontier" finding — AI-aided consultants outperformed on tasks inside the frontier, under-performed outside it, and the ability to tell was limited. The most memorable single finding for the honest-measurement discipline.
- Cui, Zheyuan (Kevin), Demirer, Mert, Jaffe, Sonia, Musolff, Leon, Peng, Sida, and Salz, Tobias — *The Effects of Generative AI on High Skilled Work: Evidence from Three Field Experiments with Software Developers*. Microsoft Research, 2024. [arXiv:2410.01331](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.01331) — task-level and experience-level heterogeneity in the productivity effect.
- METR — *Measuring the Impact of Early-2025 AI on Experienced Open-Source Developer Productivity*. [metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/) · [arXiv:2507.09089](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09089) — the surprising finding that experienced OSS developers on complex tasks felt faster with AI but were measurably slower on the specific sample. Important counterpoint to the Peng et al. positive findings.
- GitHub — *Research: quantifying GitHub Copilot's impact on developer productivity and happiness*. [github.blog/2022-09-07](https://github.blog/2022-09-07-research-quantifying-github-copilots-impact-on-developer-productivity-and-happiness/) — vendor-authored, read critically.
- Dohmke, Thomas — *GitHub Octoverse*. [github.blog/octoverse](https://github.blog/category/octoverse/) — annual GitHub ecosystem reports.
- Google DORA — *State of DevOps Report*. [dora.dev](https://dora.dev/) — the productivity-measurement discipline the AI-assisted-productivity question sits inside; recent editions cover AI-assisted development.
- Stack Overflow — *Developer Survey*. [survey.stackoverflow.co](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/) — assistant-use and satisfaction data at population scale.
- McKinsey — *The Economic Potential of Generative AI*. 2023. [mckinsey.com](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/mckinsey-digital/our-insights/the-economic-potential-of-generative-ai-the-next-productivity-frontier) — macro context; not for individual-team calibration.
