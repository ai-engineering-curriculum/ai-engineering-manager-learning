# Resources for mod-101: Role, Ladder Position, and Operating Contract

Curated references for this module. Each entry is a primary or widely-cited source — if you reach for one book to go deeper on an idea introduced in the chapters, start with the entries below.

## Engineering-management canon (assumed, not re-taught)

These are the sources chapter 02 builds on when it names the "canon" half of the operating contract. If you have not read any of them before taking a level-45 role, pick one and read a chapter before continuing with this track.

- Camille Fournier — *The Manager's Path*. [Publisher page](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/the-managers-path/9781491973882/)
- Andy Grove — *High Output Management*. [Publisher page](https://www.harpercollins.com/products/high-output-management-andrew-s-grove)
- Will Larson — *An Elegant Puzzle: Systems of Engineering Management*. [Author site](https://lethain.com/elegant-puzzle/)
- Will Larson — *Staff Engineer: Leadership Beyond the Management Track*. [Author site](https://staffeng.com/book)
- Michael Lopp — *Managing Humans* and the long-running *Rands in Repose* archive. [Rands in Repose management archive](https://randsinrepose.com/archives/category/management/)
- Google re:Work — public guides on management practice. [re:Work guides](https://rework.withgoogle.com/en/guides)
- Google Project Oxygen — the empirical findings behind re:Work. [Project Oxygen guide](https://rework.withgoogle.com/en/guides/managers-identify-what-makes-a-great-manager)
