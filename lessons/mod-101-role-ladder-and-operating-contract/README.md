# mod-101: The AI Engineering Manager — Role, Ladder Position, and the Operating Contract

> First module of the AI Engineering Manager track. Positions the role on the level ladder, reads its operating contract as the union of general engineering-management canon and AI-engineering-specific scope, diagrams the handoff contracts in every direction, and ends with a 90-day entry plan for taking over an existing team.

**Estimated effort:** 12 hours · **Role level:** 45 (AI Engineering family)

## Learning objectives

By the end of this module you should be able to:

- Position the level-45 AI Engineering Manager against `ai-augmented-team-manager` (L40, AI Workforce family), `ai-enablement-lead` (L45, sibling), `ai-infra-team-lead` (peer manager, AI Infra family), `agentic-systems-architect` and `principal-ml-engineer` (L48, staff-plus IC), and `chief-ai-officer` (L70, executive) — and name in one sentence per role what they own that you do not.
- Read the operating contract as the **union** of general engineering-management canon (Fournier, Grove, Larson, Lopp, Google re:Work, Project Oxygen) and AI-engineering-specific scope — non-deterministic delivery, eval-driven release, the model + data + safety triad, model-vendor portfolio, AI-specific incident response.
- Diagram the handoff contracts this role operates: down to the IC ladders you manage, sideways to the governance peers whose evidence you consume and the infra-team-lead peer you co-own release with, up to staff-plus ICs and the executive, out to the partners explicitly not in scope.
- Draft a defensible 90-day entry plan — team-shape audit, model + vendor inventory, eval + incident inventory, prompt + judge registry audit, stakeholder tour, three first commitments.
