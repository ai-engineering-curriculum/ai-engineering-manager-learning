# Resources for mod-108: Performance Management, Growth Paths, and Anti-Atrophy for AI Engineers

Curated references for this module. Each entry is a primary or widely-cited source. Books with multiple editions move; always check publication date and current edition against the content as it existed when this file was written (2026-11).

## Canonical engineering-management reading (prerequisite for this module)

The general management canon is `ai-augmented-team-manager` (level 40) territory and is this module's prerequisite, not its content. The references below are the canonical reading the manager is assumed to have internalised; this module specialises them to the AI engineering surface.

- Fournier, Camille — *The Manager's Path: A Guide for Tech Leaders Navigating Growth and Change*. O'Reilly, 2017. [O'Reilly page](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/the-managers-path/9781491973882/) — the canonical shape of the engineering-management career ladder, the 1:1 practice, the IC-to-manager transition, and the manager-to-senior-manager progression.
- Grove, Andrew S. — *High Output Management*. Vintage, 1995 (reissued 2015). [Vintage page](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/58715/high-output-management-by-andrew-s-grove/) — the operating-system substrate underneath performance management (1:1s, meetings, OKR-shape objectives, delegation).
- Larson, Will — *An Elegant Puzzle: Systems of Engineering Management*. Stripe Press, 2019. [Stripe Press page](https://press.stripe.com/an-elegant-puzzle) — systems thinking applied to engineering-management problems, including performance-management and calibration mechanics.
- Larson, Will — *Staff Engineer: Leadership Beyond the Management Track*. 2021. [staffeng.com](https://staffeng.com/) — the counterpart reading for the staff-plus IC track, important context for the IC ↔ manager pivot (chapter 04).
- Lopp, Michael (Rands) — *Managing Humans: Biting and Humorous Tales of a Software Engineering Manager*. 4th edition, Apress, 2019. [Apress page](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4842-7115-2) · *Rands in Repose* blog archive. [randsinrepose.com](https://randsinrepose.com/)
- Hogan, Lara — *Resilient Management*. A Book Apart, 2019. [A Book Apart page](https://abookapart.com/products/resilient-management) — manager-of-managers craft, the growth-plan surface, feedback and performance conversations.
- Zhuo, Julie — *The Making of a Manager: What to Do When Everyone Looks to You*. Portfolio, 2019. [Penguin Random House page](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/572142/the-making-of-a-manager-by-julie-zhuo/)
- Scott, Kim — *Radical Candor: Fully Revised and Updated Edition*. St. Martin's Press, 2019. [Macmillan page](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9781250235374/radicalcandor) — the two-axis feedback frame used in chapter 03.
- Horstman, Mark and Michael Auzenne — *The Effective Manager*. Wiley, 2016. [Wiley page](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/The+Effective+Manager-p-9781119244608) — practical weekly operating rhythm including structured 1:1s and feedback cadence.
- Goldsmith, Marshall — *What Got You Here Won't Get You There: How Successful People Become Even More Successful*. Hyperion, 2007. [Hachette page](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/marshall-goldsmith/what-got-you-here-wont-get-you-there/9781401384562/) — context for behavioural-change work in senior-IC and manager growth plans.

## AI-assistant and productivity-measurement research (chapter 03, chapter 05)

Empirical research on frontier assistants' effect on developer productivity, judgment, and craft. The field is young; findings differ across populations and tasks. Read broadly and update as the evidence evolves.

- Peng, Sida, Kalliamvakou, Eirini et al. — *The Impact of AI on Developer Productivity: Evidence from GitHub Copilot*. 2023. [arXiv:2302.06590](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.06590)
- GitHub — *Research: quantifying GitHub Copilot's impact on developer productivity and happiness*. [github.blog/2022-09-07](https://github.blog/2022-09-07-research-quantifying-github-copilots-impact-on-developer-productivity-and-happiness/) — vendor-authored, read critically.
- Microsoft Research — *The Effects of Generative AI on High Skilled Work: Evidence from Three Field Experiments with Software Developers*. 2024. [arXiv:2410.01331](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.01331)
- METR — *Measuring the Impact of Early-2025 AI on Experienced Open-Source Developer Productivity*. [metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/) · [arXiv:2507.09089](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09089) — notable for finding developers *felt* faster with AI assistance but were measurably slower on a specific experienced-developer-on-complex-OSS sample. Important primary reading for chapter 05's atrophy argument.
- Dohmke, Thomas — *GitHub Octoverse*. [github.blog/octoverse](https://github.blog/category/octoverse/) — annual GitHub ecosystem reports with assistant-use data.
- Stack Overflow — *Developer Survey*. [survey.stackoverflow.co](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/) — assistant-use and satisfaction data at population scale.
- Google DORA — *State of DevOps Report*. [dora.dev](https://dora.dev/) — productivity-measurement discipline the AI-assisted-productivity question sits inside.
- McKinsey — *The Economic Potential of Generative AI*. 2023. [mckinsey.com](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/mckinsey-digital/our-insights/the-economic-potential-of-generative-ai-the-next-productivity-frontier) — vendor-adjacent and macro; useful for context, not for individual-engineer calibration.
