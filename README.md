Leads teams that build AI products and agents: hiring and leveling for AI engineering roles, planning around non-deterministic work, eval-driven delivery, and managing model and vendor risk.

> **Scope:** this track is for managers of teams that *build with* models. If you manage a team whose everyday work *uses* AI (engineering or otherwise), see [AI-Augmented Team Manager](https://github.com/ai-workforce-curriculum/ai-augmented-team-manager-learning) in the AI Workforce curriculum.

> **Status**: curriculum plan published 2026-11-07. 12 planned modules (176 h) + 3 planned projects (140 h) = ~316 h — see [`CURRICULUM.md`](CURRICULUM.md) and [`.aicg/curriculum-plan.json`](.aicg/curriculum-plan.json). The requirements catalog is in `phase: "scaffold"` this cycle (live postings deferred — WebSearch / WebFetch were permission-gated); see [`JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md`](JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md). Lessons and projects will be drafted by subsequent autonomous content cycles (`aicg org daily`) once `aicg org research` attaches posting evidence.
