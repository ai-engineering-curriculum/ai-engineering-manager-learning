Leads teams that build AI products and agents: hiring and leveling for AI engineering roles, planning around non-deterministic work, eval-driven delivery, and managing model and vendor risk.

> **Scope:** this track is for managers of teams that *build with* models. If you manage a team whose everyday work *uses* AI (engineering or otherwise), see [AI-Augmented Team Manager](https://github.com/ai-workforce-curriculum/ai-augmented-team-manager-learning) in the AI Workforce curriculum.
