## Methodology

1. Sourced the canonical task domains for an AI Engineering Manager (level 45, AI Engineering family) from the public references catalogued in `.aicg/job-requirements.json → authoritative_references`.
2. Mapped each task domain to (a) the role on our level ladder that should own it primarily and (b) the curriculum module that will cover it.
3. Applied the **ownership rule** — assign coverage to the lowest-level role that genuinely requires the skill; higher-level tracks link back rather than duplicate. In particular, deferred:
   - *Down* to [`ai-augmented-team-manager`](https://github.com/ai-workforce-curriculum/ai-augmented-team-manager-learning) (level 40, AI Workforce family) for the team-scope AI-adoption fundamentals and general engineering-management rituals (1:1s, feedback, hiring loop mechanics, team roadmap mechanics) this role assumes but does not re-teach.
   - *Down* to the AI Engineering IC tracks — [`applied-ai-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/applied-ai-engineer-learning) (level 30), [`agentic-ai-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/agentic-ai-engineer-learning) (level 30), [`ai-eval-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/ai-eval-engineer-learning) (level 30), [`rag-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/rag-engineer-learning) (level 30), [`fine-tuning-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/fine-tuning-engineer-learning) (level 30), [`llm-application-developer`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/llm-application-developer-learning) (level 20), [`senior-agentic-ai-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/senior-agentic-ai-engineer-learning) (level 40) — for the engineering craft this role hires, levels, and reviews work from.
   - *Down* to [`ai-assisted-software-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/ai-assisted-software-engineer-learning) (level 20) for the IC-level AI-assisted development stack this manager commissions (R14).
   - *Sideways* to [`ai-enablement-lead`](https://github.com/ai-workforce-curriculum/ai-enablement-lead-learning) (level 45, AI Workforce family) for org-wide AI enablement (CoE, policy co-authoring, non-engineer literacy). This engineering-management role partners as a function-level customer, not as owner.
   - *Sideways* to [`agent-platform-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/agent-platform-engineer-learning) (level 40) for internal agent-platform IC build depth; to [`agentic-safety-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/agentic-safety-engineer-learning) (level 40) for frontier-agent red-team methodology; to [`forward-deployed-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/forward-deployed-engineer-learning) (level 35) for customer-embedded AI build when the team ships that shape of work.
   - *Sideways* to the AI Governance family — [`ai-risk-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-risk-engineer-learning) (level 25) for engineered red-team / adversarial-ML / guardrail depth; [`ai-evaluation-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-evaluation-engineer-learning) (level 35) for release-assurance / audit-trail / regulator-facing methodology — both produce evidence this manager consumes.
   - *Sideways* to [`ai-infra-team-lead`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-team-lead-learning) (peer manager, level 45, AI Infra ladder) for AI-infra manager scope (model serving, cluster, GPU capacity, training platform) when the team is infra-shaped. Shared management fundamentals; split by scope.
   - *Up* to [`agentic-systems-architect`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/agentic-systems-architect-learning) (level 48) and [`principal-ml-engineer`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/principal-ml-engineer-learning) (level 48) for staff-plus technical architecture scope this role partners with but does not own as IC.
   - *Up* to [`senior-ai-governance-architect`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/senior-ai-governance-architect-learning) (level 50) for control-library architecture; [`head-of-ai-governance`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/head-of-ai-governance-learning) (level 60) for governance program leadership; [`chief-ai-officer`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/chief-ai-officer-learning) (level 70) for enterprise AI strategy, portfolio P&L, board-level external positioning, and M&A.
   - *Out of scope* to legal counsel (no legal opinion), to procurement and finance (contract execution and billing mechanics), to IT identity (SSO / IAM implementation), and to security operations / DFIR (SOC-side investigation; this role feeds AI-specific signal to SecOps).
4. Flagged everything with `frequency: "needs-research"` so the next cycle can attach posting evidence without renumbering identifiers.
